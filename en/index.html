Process

How a project comes together

From idea to construction site: what happens at each stage and what you receive.

  1. 01

    Briefing

    We study the site and clarify your requirements, budget and timeline, and check planning conditions and codes.

    We need from you: site documents (cadastre), a topographic survey, the planning permit (APZ) and your wishes.

    You receive
    • Design brief
    • Site capacity study
    • List of works and stages
    Example

    We estimate in advance how many floors and apartments fit on a 0.5 ha site and where the parking can go.

  2. 02

    Concept: sketches and 3D

    We develop massing, plan and facade options, the site plan and technical and economic indicators (TEP), and show it all in 3D.

    You receive
    • Site plan and TEP
    • Plan and facade options
    • 3D model, day and night renders
    • Presentation album
    Example

    Two or three massing options for the same site: one with more apartments, another with a larger courtyard and more greenery. You compare the TEP figures and 3D views side by side and choose.

  3. 03

    Design and expert review

    All sections are developed from the chosen option: architecture, structures, water and sewerage, HVAC, electrical and cost estimate. We prepare everything for state expert review and approvals.

    You receive
    • AR, KJ/KM, VK, OV, EOM, SS, GP sections
    • Explanatory note and cost estimate
    • Package for expert review
    Example

    One team does every section, so structures and engineering systems are coordinated with the architecture from the start — fewer review comments.

  4. 04

    Working drawings

    Detailed drawings builders work from: details, reinforcement, specifications and schedules.

    You receive
    • Details
    • Rebar and steel specifications
    • Door, window, finish and floor schedules
    • Set in PDF and DWG
    Example

    Facade details, stair and lift-shaft reinforcement, door and window schedules — so builders can work without extra questions.

  5. 05

    Designer’s supervision

    We support the project during construction: site visits, answers to questions and updates to the drawings.

    You receive
    • Site visits
    • Answers to builders’ questions
    • Drawing revisions
    Example

    If a window size or material changes on site, we quickly update the relevant drawings and schedules.

Whatever stage your project is at, we can pick it up from there.
